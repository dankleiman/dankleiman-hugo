One thing that's been on my mind since [Bruce was here teaching a Push Hands Intensive](/taichi/push-hands-and-the-tai-chi-mastery-program/ "Push Hands and The Tai Chi Mastery Program") is what it means to follow instructions in your training. He basically laid out a year or longer curriculum during a week. What do you come away with? What should you practice? How do you reconcile "downloading" a whole curriculum at once vs. really upacking it and learning to use it over the next year.

These are interesting problems to have, right?  
  
I discuss the psychology of following instructions in Episode 4 of Qigong Radio.
